EMBODIED BODY / WHEELED MOBILE MANIPULATOR

FWMiniPiper

A four-wheel-steering mobile base with a Piper arm and gripper. A front camera supports navigation; a wrist camera follows the arm for close-range manipulation.

MORPHOLOGY & MANIPULATION

Body configuration

Download body configuration ↗

A mobile manipulation platform combining a four-wheel-steering chassis, a six-joint Piper arm, a parallel gripper and a cargo basket. The base brings the arm within reach for pickup, delivery and object interaction.

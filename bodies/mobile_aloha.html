EMBODIED BODY / DUAL-ARM WHEELED MANIPULATOR

Mobile ALOHA

Two articulated arms and parallel grippers on a wheeled mobile base, with overhead and wrist cameras for mobile manipulation.

MORPHOLOGY & MANIPULATION

Body configuration

Download body configuration ↗

A differential-drive mobile manipulator with two six-axis ViperX arms and two coupled parallel grippers. This benchmark adaptation combines ALOHA 2 arm assets with manufacturer CAD for the AgileX TRACER base and Mobile ALOHA frame.

6 × 2arm joints
16active actuators
3RGB cameras

Mobile base

Two independently driven wheels produce forward travel and turning through physical ground contact.

Drive
Differential · non-holonomic
Wheel radius / track
0.0605 m / 0.34 m
Command limits
0.5 m/s · 0.7 rad/s
Supporting contacts
Four low-friction support balls; caster approximation

Dual arms & grippers

Both six-axis arms can move independently or through a synchronized joint trajectory. Each parallel gripper uses one control input and coupled finger joints.

Arm source
MuJoCo Menagerie · ALOHA 2 / ViperX 300
Arm actuators
12 position inputs
Gripper actuators
2 position inputs · metres
Wheel actuators
2 velocity inputs

Control & interaction

Bounded inverse kinematics and measured contact evidence support reaching, grasping, lifting and pressing.

Physics / control
MuJoCo · 500 Hz / 50 Hz
IK reference frame
Live base · X forward / Y left / Z up
Navigation
Relative XY · sensor-grid A* · transport posture required
Success evidence
Measured tracking error, opposed contacts and actual lift

Simulation adaptation, not an exact replica or hardware-calibrated digital twin. Base and frame visuals use AgileX CAD; collision shapes and passive caster dynamics are approximations; arm gravity compensation is enabled. Demonstration animation is a joint articulation study, not a benchmark success claim.
